### 🏆 1. Sahayak — Multilingual Agentic Citizen Companion

> A multilingual, agentic AI system designed to help citizens discover government schemes and transform natural-language queries into grounded, actionable plans.

**Core Capabilities:**

- Stateful 5-agent architecture: Understanding, Research, Planning, Critic and Replanner.
- Multilingual citizen query processing in English, Hindi, Malayalam and Tamil.
- Advanced hybrid RAG combining ChromaDB, BM25, Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) and Cross-Encoder reranking.
- Knowledge retrieval across 34 government documents and 149 chunks.
- Improved top-1 retrieval accuracy from 33% to 100%.
- NLP-based intent, emotional tone and life-event extraction.
- JWT authentication and role-based access control with PostgreSQL.

### 📊 3. RetainIQ — Telecom Customer Churn & Retention Intelligence

> An intelligent customer retention platform combining machine learning, explainable AI and LLM-powered recommendations to identify churn risks and support customer retention decisions.

**Core Capabilities:**

- XGBoost and LightGBM ensemble for customer churn prediction.
- Achieved 0.88 ROC-AUC and 0.68 F1-score using stratified 5-fold cross-validation.
- Multi-format data ingestion with automatic column mapping.
- SHA-256-based data versioning.
- Hot-swap model retraining using FastAPI and SQLAlchemy.
- SHAP-based per-customer model explainability.
- Bilingual English and Arabic LLM retention advisor grounded in policy.
